## 🏛️ Portfolio Architecture & Chambers

This portfolio is structured as an industrial architectural exhibition divided into 11 distinct computational chambers:

1. **Chamber 01: Hero & Kinetic Frame Canvas** — Pre-rendered 120-frame scroll sequence canvas paired with zero-lag scene transitions.
2. **Structural Capability Datum Bar** — Highlighting core engineering competencies (Multi-Agent Graphs, Hybrid RAG, React 19 / FastAPI, 6 Scholar Papers, Playwright QA).
3. **Chamber 02: Spatial Anatomy & Principal Biography** — Sai Gaddam's philosophy, verified credentials at SR University ML Lab, and telemetry coordinates.
4. **Chamber 03: Commissioned Engineering Disciplines** — Service scopes across AI Agents, Knowledge Systems, Full-Stack, Generative AI, and QA Automation.
5. **Chamber 04: The Artifact Archive & System Dossiers** — Production and research case studies (Autonomous QA Platform, Multimodal Emotion Detection, Depression Prediction, Telugu NMT, RythuMitra).
6. **Chamber 05: Computational 3D Simulation Chamber** — Interactive Three.js topologies demonstrating Agent cognition cycles, RAG ingestion, and scalable cloud stacks.
7. **Chamber 06: Technical Expertise & Matrix** — Structured proficiency index across Artificial Intelligence, Web Systems, Data & Infrastructure.
8. **Chamber 07: Empirical Crypt & Google Scholar Publications** — 6 peer-reviewed research papers with interactive BibTeX copy and Scholar links.
9. **Chamber 08: The 5-Phase Execution Blueprint** — Transparent sprint methodology from discovery to production handover.
10. **Chamber 09 & 10: Client Blueprint & Testimonials** — Freelance contracts, guarantees, and verified client feedback.
11. **Chamber 11: The Transmission Chamber** — Inquiry form with dual-currency budgeting (USD / INR), timeline selector, and direct mail dispatch.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Core Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript
- **Bundler & Build**: Vite + Rolldown / Oxc
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 + Custom Industrial Architectural Design System
- **3D Graphics & Animations**: Three.js, GSAP, HTML5 Canvas
- **Icons & Effects**: Lucide React, Canvas Confetti, Web Audio API Sound System
- **Fonts**: Space Grotesk, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono
